Continue feeding every 2–3 hours

Feed sooner when baby shows hunger cues. Don’t try to stretch sleep in week one.

  • Keep lights lowHelp distinguish night from day.

  • Keep care quietFeed, burp, change if needed.

  • Back to safe sleepOn baby’s back, firm flat surface, clear crib.
